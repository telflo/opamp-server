# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and reviews, and
wherever someone represents the project.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, disagree about the work and not
the person, and accept a maintainer's decision on what belongs here.

**Reporting.** Email jason@telflo.com with what happened, where, and
who was involved. Reports are read only by the maintainers, kept
confidential, and answered. The maintainers act on them as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe, from a warning to a permanent ban.
